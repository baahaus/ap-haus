The description Google shows is for another town

Meta description

What we found

Live on ultimetfitness.com / meta name="description""Discover the best gym in Jupiter. Get stronger and move better with expert coaching and flexible programs."

Why it matters

That sentence is not decoration. It is the line a person reads underneath your name in Google before they decide whether to click. Right now it names Jupiter, Florida, which is where the theme this site was built on came from. It was never rewritten. Anyone searching "gym south side pittsburgh" sees a result that appears to be about somewhere else.

The fix

Rewrite it around where you actually are and what you actually do, in under 155 characters, with South Side or Pittsburgh in the first half. It is a five minute change with an outsized effect on click-through.
