The description gets cut mid list

Meta description

What we found

Live on idassandwichshop.com / meta descriptionLength: 216 characters
Google shows roughly 155

Visible: "Ida's Sandwich Shop is a cafe in the neighborhood of Observatory Hill on the North Side of Pittsburgh with the best deli sandwiches, hamburgers, fish..."
sandwiches, hot sandwiches, Fresh cut fries, salads, soups, halal.

Why it matters

Everything after the cut is invisible in search. That includes fresh cut fries and halal, and halal in particular is a word people search for on purpose. Right now it is the last word in a 216 character tag, which is the one place it is guaranteed not to be seen.

The fix

Rewrite to about 150 characters and put the differentiators first: halal deli sandwiches, burgers and fresh cut fries on Perrysville Avenue in Observatory Hill.

Why it matters

Nobody who has not already heard of you is searching Ida's Sandwich Shop. They are searching halal food near me, or deli North Side Pittsburgh. Those words are in your description, which is truncated, and not in your title, which is the line that actually ranks.

The fix
