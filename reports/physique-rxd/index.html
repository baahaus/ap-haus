01/05

Two addresses, one footer, one line apart

Local signals

What we found

Live on physiquerxd.com / site footer"PHYSIQUERXD, 835 WESTERN AVE, PITTSBURGH, PA, 15233, UNITED STATES"

"502 West North Avenue Pittsburgh, PA 15212"

Both printed on every page. Different street, different zip.

Why it matters

Somebody deciding whether to book an intro class has to work out which of the two addresses is real, and they are in different zip codes. Google has the same problem: when your site, your Facebook page and your Yelp listing disagree about the address, local search treats the business as less trustworthy and ranks it lower. This is the single most expensive line of text on the site.

The fix

Pick the current address, put it in one place, and make sure the same string appears on Google Business, Facebook and Yelp. Then add LocalBusiness structured data so there is a machine readable answer.

Your email is printed with www in front of it

Contact details

What we found

Correct: physique.rxd@gmail.com
Clickable mailto link: no

Why it matters

An email address with www in front of it is not an email address. Anyone who copies that line and pastes it into their mail app gets a bounce, and there is no clickable mailto anywhere on the page to fall back on. That is a booking lost at the last step, and you never hear about it.

The fix

Drop the www, and make it a real mailto link so a phone can open it with one tap.
