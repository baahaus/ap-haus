H1 headings on page: 1
H2 headings on page: 0

Why it matters

The H1 is the one line search engines treat as the subject of a page. Yours is a divider that was styled as a heading by accident, so the strongest structural signal on your homepage says nothing. Underneath it there are zero H2 headings, so the sections a visitor can clearly see are invisible to a crawler.

The fix

One real H1 that names the category and the place, something like "Curated Flame / Smoke and CBD Shop in Millvale." Then turn the section labels you already have on the page into proper H2s. The look does not need to change.

Your title tag is two words long

Title tag

What we found

Live on curatedflamepgh.com / <title>"Curated Flame" , 13 characters

Your own meta description says:
"...Nominated the last two years for best cbd shop & best head shop in Pittsburgh."

Why it matters

The title is the biggest text in a google result and the only thing shown in a browser tab. Yours carries no category and no geography, so a search for "smoke shop pittsburgh" or "cbd millvale" has nothing to match against. Two Pittsburgh award nominations sit in your description and never make it into the line people actually read first.

The fix

Keep it under 60 characters and put the category and the neighborhood in it. Those award nominations are a real differentiator and belong in the visible copy, not buried in a meta tag.

The share preview is a logo, served over plain http

Link previews

What we found

Live on curatedflamepgh.com / og:image + twitter:cardog:image = http://static1.squarespace.com/.../Square+Transparent.png
twitter:card = summary (small square thumbnail)

Why it matters

Two things here. The image is your transparent logo rather than the shop, so a shared link looks like a file attachment instead of a storefront. And the URL is plain http on an otherwise https site, which some apps refuse to load at all, leaving a blank box.

The fix

Point og:image at a real https photo of the shop, sized around 1200x630, and switch twitter:card to summary_large_image so the preview renders wide instead of as a thumbnail.
