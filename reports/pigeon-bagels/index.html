Total readable text on the homepage: 321 characters

Why it matters

Headings are how a page tells Google what it is about, and how a screen reader lets someone jump around it. Yours has none, at any level. Combined with 321 characters of text total, the homepage of a kosher bakery people drive across the city for gives a search engine almost nothing to rank. Everything you would want to rank for, hand-rolled, kosher, Squirrel Hill, is on the page as picture or navigation, not as text.

The fix

One H1 that says Pigeon Bagels, hand-rolled kosher bagels in Squirrel Hill. Two or three H2s over short blocks about the bagels, the pastry case and wholesale. It does not have to change the look.
