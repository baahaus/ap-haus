An icon's file name is printed as text in your address heading

Page structure

"Visit us at
736 Bellefonte Street, Pittsburgh, PA 15232

Visible to visitors and to Google: yes

Why it matters

Your site builder puts a small pin graphic next to the address and its internal name, map marker pin, is leaking out as real text inside the heading. It reads as a typo to anyone who notices, and a screen reader announces it out loud in the middle of your address. It is a two second fix that has been sitting there.

The fix

Render the pin as an icon with no text, or give it alt text of an empty string so it stops being read as content.

Mentions sushi: no
Mentions Shadyside: no

Why it matters

The grey line under your search result is the one sentence you get to persuade someone. It currently leads with a hard coded price, never says the word sushi, and never says Shadyside. It also has to be edited by hand every time the price changes, and a stale price in a search result is worse than none.

The fix

All you can eat sushi and Japanese small plates on Bellefonte Street in Shadyside. Dine in, takeout and reservations for any size party. That ranks, and it does not go stale.

Why it matters

One H1 is the right number, so that part is correct. But it repeats the name and location that are already in the title tag and the address block, so it uses your most valuable line of text to say nothing new. Underneath it, seventeen H2s carry sentences like Make Some Memories, which is atmosphere, not something anyone searches for.

The fix

Put the offer in the H1: All You Can Eat Sushi in Shadyside. Keep the name in the title tag where it already is.
