Why it matters

Not one heading tag on the homepage. Search engines use headings to work out what a page is and how it is organised, so yours reads as a single undifferentiated block. Ten of the fifteen photos also have no alt text, so they contribute nothing.

The fix

An H1 naming the tavern, the town and the category, H2s over the sections you already have, and a short alt line on each photo.
