01/04

There is no H1 on the page

Page structure

What we found

Live on piccolo-forno.com / heading countH1 headings on page: 0
H2 headings on page: 13

Why it matters

The H1 is the single line search engines read as the subject of a page. Yours does not exist, so google is left to guess what the homepage is about from your navigation and body copy. The 13 H2s underneath are all doing the visual work of headings with nothing above them to anchor to.

The fix

One real H1 near the top, something like "Piccolo Forno / Wood Fired Tuscan Cooking in Lawrenceville." Nothing about the look of the page has to change, the tag just needs to exist.

02/04

No restaurant data for google to read

Local signals

What we found

Live on piccolo-forno.com / structured dataJSON-LD blocks found: 0
Restaurant schema: none
Hours, price range, menu link, address: not marked up

Why it matters

Structured data is how google gets hours, price range, menu and reservations into the panel next to your listing. With none on the page, all of that has to be scraped or pulled from third party sites instead, which is why aggregators often outrank a restaurant for its own name.

The fix

Add a Restaurant schema block with your address, phone, hours, price range and a link to the menu. It is invisible to visitors and takes one pass to write.

03/04

Shared links have no card format set

Link previews

What we found

Live on piccolo-forno.com / head tagsog:image = present
twitter:card = missing
canonical tag = missing

Why it matters

You do have a share image, which is good. Without a twitter:card tag it renders as a small square thumbnail rather than a wide photo on X and in several messaging apps. The missing canonical tag means google decides on its own whether piccolo-forno.com and www.piccolo-forno.com are the same page.

The fix

Set twitter:card to summary_large_image and add a canonical tag pointing at the version of the domain you want indexed. Ten minutes of work.
