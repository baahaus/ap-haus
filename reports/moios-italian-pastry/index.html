The title names Cranberry Township, not Monroeville

Meta title

What we found

Live on moios.com / title tag<title>

Contains "Monroeville": no

Why it matters

That title is the blue line in every Google result and the name of the browser tab. It currently advertises Cranberry Township, which is about 30 miles north of your counter, and never mentions the town you have actually been in since 1972. Anyone searching Italian bakery Monroeville is being handed a result that says Cranberry.

The fix

Rewrite it to something like Moio's Italian Pastry Shop | Monroeville, PA | Cakes and Cannoli Since 1935. Same length, correct town, and it names what you sell.

03/05

Your share image tag is set to the word home

Link previews

What we found

Live on moios.com / open graph tagsog:image = "home"
https://www.moios.com/images/...

Valid image URL: no
twitter:card = summary (small thumbnail)

Why it matters

An open graph image tag has to hold a full URL to a picture. Yours holds the word home, so there is nothing for Facebook or a text message to load. Every time someone shares a link to your cake gallery, it renders blank. A bakery is the most photogenic business on the internet and your links show nothing.

The fix

Point og:image at a real photo of the case, and switch twitter:card to summary_large_image so the preview renders wide instead of as a stamp.

Why it matters

Structured data is good, and you have it, which already puts you ahead. But the shop is declared as a LocalBusiness four times on one page. Google has to pick one and guess, and duplicate blocks can suppress the rich result you are supposed to be earning from having the markup in the first place.

The fix

Collapse to one Bakery block with the Monroeville address, hours, phone and price range. Keep the FAQ markup, it is doing real work.
