There is no structured data on the site at all

Why it matters

Structured data is a small hidden block that spells out the basics in a format search engines read directly rather than guess at. It is what powers the hours panel, the map result and the "open now" line on a phone. Most restaurant sites built on a platform get this for free. Yours has none, so Google is inferring everything about a bar that only opened in October 2024 and has the least history to go on.

The fix

Add a Restaurant block with hours, address, price range and a link to the menu. One-time addition, and it is the highest-leverage thing on this list.

The description gets cut off mid-sentence

Meta description

What we found

Live on solerapgh.com, 325 characters"Our menu is a carefully selected list of wines from around the globe. To make sure that you're paying for the juice in the bottle and not the name on the label, we often bring in wines from regions and producers you may not be familiar with... [170 more characters]"

Why it matters

That is a good line, and it is your actual philosophy. Google prints about 155 characters, so a searcher reads up to "not" and then it stops. The half that carries the point never gets shown. It also never says Lawrenceville, which is the word most people are typing.

The fix

One sentence under 155 characters, leading with wine bar and Lawrenceville, keeping the juice-not-the-label idea.

Why it matters

Fifteen top-level headings means none of them count for anything. The placeholder files are the images the platform ships with before real photos are added, and a few are still on the page. And fourteen of your photos carry no description, so the wine and the room are invisible to image search.

The fix

One H1, real photos in place of the placeholders, and short descriptions on the rest.
