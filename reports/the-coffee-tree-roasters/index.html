No meta description

Search snippet

What we found

Title: Coffee Tree Roasters

Why it matters

The description is the two lines of sales copy under your name in every search result. Without one, Google scrapes text off the page, and the roaster gets described by accident rather than on purpose.

The fix

Write one line naming the roastery, the cafes and what makes the coffee yours.

Why it matters

Your structured data is actually good, which makes the missing headline more frustrating. The single most important heading on the page does not exist, so the strongest on page signal you own is empty.

The fix

One H1 naming the roaster and the city, with the rest of the page hung underneath it.

03/04

Your share image is a logo lockup served over http

Link previews

What we found

Checked 08.05.26og:image: Lockup 2.png
Served over: http, not https
twitter:card: summary (small square)

Why it matters

Two problems in one tag. The image is a logo rather than coffee, and it is referenced over plain http, which some platforms refuse to load at all. That combination is why shared links look empty.

The fix

A wide https photo of the roastery or a pour, plus summary_large_image.

04/04

The domain and the email do not match

Brand signals

What we found

Checked 08.05.26Live site: coffeetree.store
Published email: custserv@coffeetree.com
Pages in sitemap: 60

Why it matters

Customers see one name in the address bar and a different one in your email. Search engines treat domain consistency as a trust signal, and people simply get confused about which one is real.

The fix

Decide which domain is the front door and point the other at it permanently.
