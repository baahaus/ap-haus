  / 3337 Penn Avenue

Why it matters

This is the hidden block that tells Google your hours, your address and what kind of place you are, in a format it reads directly instead of guessing at. It drives the hours panel, the map result and the "open now" tag. Your hours are unusual, closed some weekdays, opening at 8, and that is exactly the case where guessing goes wrong and somebody drives over on a closed day.

The fix

Add a Restaurant block with the real schedule. One-time job, and it is the single biggest gap here.

Why it matters

Those three words are the internal name of a location icon that leaked into the text. A screen reader says them out loud before your address, and Google indexes them as part of it. On top of that, fifteen separate H1 headings means the page has no single main heading at all.

The fix

Pull the icon out of the heading, keep one H1, demote the rest. No visual change.
