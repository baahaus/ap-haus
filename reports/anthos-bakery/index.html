Your description tag is empty

Length: 0 characters

Why it matters

The tag exists but is blank, so google writes your search snippet itself by grabbing whatever text it finds first. You have no say in the one sentence that decides whether somebody clicks. The title also opens with the word "Home," which spends your most valuable characters on nothing.

The fix

Write a single 150 character sentence naming Greek bakery, Castle Shannon and what you are known for. Then rewrite the title to lead with the category and the neighborhood.

Why it matters

With no og:image tag, every text, Facebook post and Instagram bio link pointing at your site renders as a grey rectangle with a url in it. For a bakery, where the whole sell is how the food looks, that is the worst possible default.

The fix

Add og:image, og:title and og:description pointing at a real 1200x630 photo of your product, plus a Bakery schema block with your address and hours.
