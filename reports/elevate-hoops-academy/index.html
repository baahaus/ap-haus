The main heading is the SEO title, pasted onto the page

H1

What we found

Homepage H1, live"Basketball Training in Pittsburgh|Elevate Hoops Academy"

Identical to the browser tab title. No space either side of the pipe character.

Why it matters

A parent landing on the page reads that line first. It reads like a file name, because it is the search phrase and the business name jammed together with a pipe. Search engines do not reward repeating the title tag word for word as the H1 either, so it is not even buying you anything.

The fix

Write the H1 for the parent, something in Coach Jay's voice, and let the title tag stay the keyword-shaped one. Keep Sharpsburg in it.
