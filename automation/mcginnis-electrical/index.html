Why it matters

Industrial and commercial work comes in by phone, and the calls that matter are outages and failures that do not wait for business hours. Right now a facility manager with a dead panel at 6pm gets voicemail, and your fifty years of experience never enters the conversation.

The opportunity

An intake assistant that answers the phone and the form the moment they land, captures the site, the system and the urgency, and either pages your on call number or books the visit. Your existing call volume turns into more booked work without spending a cent on advertising.
