Why it matters

Families do not shop around. Whoever answers, calmly and immediately, at 3am, is the funeral home they use. There is no second chance and no price comparison. The call is the whole business.

The opportunity

An assistant that answers every call instantly, with the right tone, takes the essential details, and pages the on call director means no family ever hears a ring out. It also means whoever is on call arrives already knowing the name, the location and the faith tradition.
