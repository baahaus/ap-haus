Why it matters

Homeowners requesting remodeling quotes almost never request just one. The company that replies first sets the terms, and everybody after that is negotiating against them. The form does not care what time it is, but the person reading it does.

The opportunity

An assistant replies within seconds, asks the qualifying questions your team asks anyway, and books the in home appointment while the homeowner is still on the page.
