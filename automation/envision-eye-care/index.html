Why it matters

Your own FAQ tells you what people ask: what you offer, how often to come in, whether you take their insurance, whether they can order through you. Those are the same four questions every time, and they arrive at all hours, most of them when the office is shut.

The opportunity

An assistant that answers those four questions instantly and books the appointment captures people at the moment they decide. The bigger win is recall. Patients who are overdue for an exam are the cheapest appointments you will ever fill, and chasing them is exactly the job nobody has time for.
