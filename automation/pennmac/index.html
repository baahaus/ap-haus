Why it matters

The tray and ring business is the part of the store that runs on deadlines, and the deadline is enforced by whoever picks up the phone. On top of that, the page that represents a hundred year old Strip District institution introduces itself to Google as PennMac Home with no description, and the featured navigation is still selling Easter in August.

The opportunity

An assistant can hold the tray rules, take the details in the right order, and stop the Friday afternoon calls that cannot be filled anyway. The site fixes are small and separate.

02/04

What an assistant would actually do

How it would work
01
Knows the tray rules

Pickup only, Thursday 11am for weekends, 48 hours during the week, ready at 11am. Answered correctly every time, including at night.

02
Takes the order details

Tray type, size, count, pickup day and time, name and number, in the order your counter would ask for them.

03
Answers the stock questions

Which cheeses, which meats, what comes on the ring, what is in the case this week.

04
Escalates the odd ones

Large catering, special requests and anything outside the rules go to a person with the details already gathered.

In plain terms

Every call that ends in someone repeating the Thursday rule is a call that did not need a person. Every call that comes in on Friday for a Saturday tray is a customer disappointed by a rule they could not find. Both are fixable with the same piece of work.

  • Tray and ring order assistant. Holds the deadline rules, collects the order, hands the unusual ones to staff.
