Why it matters

The alt text and the missing description are cheap fixes. The expensive gap is the phone. Families call once, at whatever hour it happens, and whoever answers properly is the funeral home they use. A ring out at 2am is a service lost with no way to know it happened.

The opportunity

An assistant that answers every first call immediately, in the right tone, gathers the details and pages your director means no family ever waits. It also handles the constant routine calls about service times so nobody gets pulled out of an arrangement.
