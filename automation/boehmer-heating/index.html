Why it matters

A furnace dies at 11pm on the coldest night of the year and the homeowner calls whoever answers. On that same night your team is already out on calls. The result is that the busiest hours of your busiest week are the hours you are least able to pick up.

The opportunity

An assistant answers all of them at once, sorts the no heat emergencies from the thermostat questions, and only wakes the on call tech for the real ones.

In plain terms

You do not need more leads. Ninety three years already produced them. You need the surge nights to stop leaking calls to whoever answered faster, and you need routine work to stop competing with emergencies for the same person.
