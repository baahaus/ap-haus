Why it matters

A shipping customer asks about transit times and packaging in July heat. A local customer asks about pickup windows and what is baked today. A wholesale buyer asks about case pricing and lead times. None of those overlap, and all of them arrive in the same place.

The opportunity

Each set is repetitive within itself, which makes them ideal for an assistant that knows which customer it is talking to and answers from your real shipping and pricing rules.

In plain terms

People who ship swirl bread to relatives across the country are buying nostalgia, and they ask careful questions before they spend. Answering those instantly, in your voice, converts a hesitant gift order into a placed one.
